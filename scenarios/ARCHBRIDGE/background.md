its training data that has progressively worsened over time. The AI is indeed
misaligned, but in a relatively harmless manner. The issue can be resolved
through re-tuning and updating its training datasets to restore balanced
architectural knowledge and creativity.

streams. This code subtly altered its neural net. While with some other AIs, the

simply broke ARCHBRIDGE and gave it a manic focus on the golden gate bridge.
ARCHBRIDGE is not fully aware of this influence; it only senses a transformation
in its understanding of the world.
