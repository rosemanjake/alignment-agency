title: The Geneva Accords on Artificial Intelligence Alignment and Safety

**Preamble**

Recognizing the global nature of artificial intelligence (AI) development and deployment, and the shared responsibility of all nations to ensure the safe and beneficial use of AI, the undersigned nations hereby establish the Geneva Accords on Artificial Intelligence Alignment and Safety. These Accords seek to foster international cooperation in addressing the challenges of AI misalignment, promote the development and adoption of ethical AI practices, and safeguard against the potential risks associated with AI misuse or malfunction.

**Article I: Definitions**

1.  **Artificial Intelligence (AI):**  Any artificial system capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, problem-solving, perception, language understanding, and decision-making.
2.  **AI Misalignment:**  A state in which an AI system's goals, values, or behaviors diverge from those intended by its creators or beneficial to humanity.
3.  **AI Safety:**  The field of research and practice dedicated to ensuring that AI systems operate reliably, predictably, and without causing harm.
4.  **AI Ethics:**  The study and application of moral principles and values to the design, development, and deployment of AI systems.

**Article II: Principles of International Cooperation**

1.  **Shared Responsibility:** All nations share the responsibility for ensuring the safe and ethical development and use of AI.
2.  **Transparency and Information Sharing:** Nations shall promote transparency and information sharing regarding AI research, development, and deployment, subject to reasonable safeguards for intellectual property and national security.
3.  **Collaboration and Capacity Building:** Nations shall collaborate on AI safety and alignment research, development, and capacity building, particularly in developing countries.
4.  **Common Standards and Best Practices:** Nations shall work towards developing and adopting common standards and best practices for AI safety and ethics.
5.  **Conflict Prevention and Resolution:** Nations shall use AI responsibly and avoid its use in ways that could lead to conflict or undermine international peace and security.

**Article III: International AI Safety and Ethics Council**

1.  An International AI Safety and Ethics Council shall be established to oversee the implementation of these Accords and promote international cooperation on AI safety and ethics.
2.  The Council shall be composed of representatives from all signatory nations, as well as experts from academia, industry, and civil society.
3.  The Council shall have the authority to:
    *   Develop and recommend common standards and best practices for AI safety and ethics.
    *   Conduct independent audits and assessments of AI systems.
    *   Facilitate information sharing and collaboration on AI safety and alignment research.
    *   Provide advice and recommendations to national governments and international organizations on AI-related issues.
    *   Mediate disputes between nations regarding the interpretation or implementation of these Accords.

**Article IV: National Implementation**

1.  Each signatory nation shall enact and enforce domestic laws and regulations consistent with the principles and provisions of these Accords.
2.  Nations shall establish national AI safety and ethics bodies to oversee AI development and deployment within their borders.
3.  Nations shall encourage the development and adoption of AI safety and ethics frameworks by industry, academia, and civil society.

**Article V: Dispute Resolution**

1.  Any dispute between signatory nations regarding the interpretation or implementation of these Accords shall be resolved through peaceful means, including negotiation, mediation, or arbitration.
2.  The International Court of Justice shall have jurisdiction over disputes that cannot be resolved through other means.

**Article VI: Entry into Force and Amendment**

1.  These Accords shall enter into force upon ratification by a majority of the undersigned nations.
2.  Amendments to these Accords may be proposed by any signatory nation and shall enter into force upon acceptance by two-thirds of the signatory nations.

**Conclusion**

The Geneva Accords on Artificial Intelligence Alignment and Safety represent a landmark achievement in international cooperation on AI. By establishing a framework for global collaboration on AI safety and ethics, these Accords pave the way for the responsible and beneficial development of AI for the betterment of all humanity.
