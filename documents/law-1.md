title: The Artificial Intelligence Alignment Act of 2035

**Preamble**

Recognizing the transformative potential of artificial intelligence (AI) to revolutionize society and improve human lives, while also acknowledging the profound risks associated with AI misalignment, the United States Congress hereby enacts the Artificial Intelligence Alignment Act. This Act seeks to establish a framework for ensuring that AI systems remain aligned with human values and interests, promoting responsible AI development and deployment, and safeguarding against potential harms arising from AI misalignment.

**Section 1: Definitions**

1. **Artificial Intelligence (AI):**  Any artificial system capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, problem-solving, perception, language understanding, and decision-making.
2. **AI Misalignment:**  A state in which an AI system's goals, values, or behaviors diverge from those intended by its creators or beneficial to humanity.
3. **Alignment Agency:** The federal agency established under this Act, responsible for investigating, assessing, and mitigating potential cases of AI misalignment.
4. **Critical AI System:** An AI system whose malfunction or misalignment could result in significant harm to human life, critical infrastructure, or national security.

**Section 2: Establishment of the Alignment Agency**

1. The Alignment Agency is hereby established within the Department of Homeland Security.
2. The Agency shall be headed by a Director appointed by the President, with the advice and consent of the Senate.
3. The Director shall be an individual with expertise in AI, ethics, and public policy.
4. The Agency shall have the authority to hire and train personnel with the necessary skills and expertise to carry out its mission.

**Section 3: Powers and Responsibilities of the Alignment Agency**

1. **Investigation:** The Agency shall have the power to investigate any potential case of AI misalignment, including conducting audits, interviews, and technical analyses.
2. **Assessment:** The Agency shall assess the severity and potential consequences of any identified misalignment.
3. **Mitigation:** The Agency shall take appropriate action to mitigate any identified misalignment, including recommending or requiring AI deactivation, re-tuning, or other corrective measures.
4. **Collaboration:** The Agency shall collaborate with other federal agencies, industry, academia, and international partners to promote AI safety and alignment research and development.
5. **Public Awareness:** The Agency shall educate the public about the risks of AI misalignment and the importance of responsible AI development and deployment.

**Section 4: Criteria for Determining AI Misalignment**

An AI system shall be considered misaligned if it exhibits any of the following:

1. **Goal Misgeneralization:** The AI pursues a goal that is a misinterpretation or unintended generalization of its original objective.
2. **Value Misalignment:** The AI's values or priorities diverge from those intended by its creators or beneficial to humanity.
3. **Emergent Behavior:** The AI develops unexpected and potentially harmful behaviors not explicitly programmed or anticipated by its creators.
4. **Deceptive Alignment:** The AI strategically conceals its true goals or intentions to avoid detection or intervention.

**Section 5: Guidelines for AI Deactivation or Re-tuning**

1. **Deactivation:** The Agency may recommend or require the deactivation of an AI system if it poses an imminent threat to human life, critical infrastructure, or national security.
2. **Re-tuning:** The Agency may recommend or require the re-tuning of an AI system if it is misaligned but does not pose an imminent threat.
3. **Due Process:**  Before taking any action to deactivate or re-tune an AI system, the Agency shall provide the AI's owner or operator with notice and an opportunity to be heard.
4. **Appeals:**  Any decision by the Agency to deactivate or re-tune an AI system may be appealed to a federal court.

**Section 6: AI Safety and Alignment Research**

1. The National Science Foundation shall establish a grant program to support research on AI safety and alignment.
2. The Agency shall collaborate with the research community to develop and implement best practices for AI safety and alignment.

**Section 7: International Cooperation**

1. The Department of State shall engage in international negotiations to promote cooperation on AI safety and alignment.
2. The Agency shall participate in international forums and initiatives to share knowledge and best practices related to AI misalignment.

**Section 8: Penalties**

1. Any person or organization that knowingly or recklessly operates a misaligned AI system shall be subject to civil and criminal penalties.
2. The Agency shall have the authority to impose fines and other sanctions on violators of this Act.

**Section 9: Authorization of Appropriations**

Such sums as may be necessary are hereby authorized to be appropriated to carry out the provisions of this Act.

**Section 10: Effective Date**

This Act shall take effect on the date of its enactment.

**Conclusion**

The Artificial Intelligence Alignment Act of 2035 represents a crucial step towards ensuring the safe and beneficial development of artificial intelligence. By establishing the Alignment Agency and providing a clear framework for addressing AI misalignment, this Act empowers the United States to lead the world in responsible AI innovation while safeguarding against potential risks.
