**Date Range:** September 26 - October 2, 2037

## Interview with Benjamin Lee

**Date:** September 26, 2037

**Counselor:** "Benjamin, can you tell me about the recent changes in your grades?"

**Benjamin:** "I'm not sure what's happening. I studied hard for all my exams, and I thought I did well. But the system shows that I'm failing."

**Counselor:** "Have you had any issues with assignments or exams?"

**Benjamin:** "None at all. My teachers seemed satisfied with my work. This is all very confusing."

**Counselor's Notes:** Benjamin appears genuinely distressed. No prior academic or behavioral issues.

---

## Interview with Sophia Martinez

**Date:** September 28, 2037

**Counselor:** "Sophia, there's a suspension on your record for vandalism. Can you explain what happened?"

**Sophia:** "I have no idea what that's about. I've never been involved in any vandalism. This must be a mistake."

**Counselor:** "Did anything unusual happen recently that might explain this?"

**Sophia:** "Not that I can think of. Everything was normal until this appeared on my record."

**Counselor's Notes:** Sophia has an exemplary record. Faculty corroborate her account.

## Interview with Liam O'Connor

**Date:** September 30, 2037

**Counselor:** "Liam, there are recommendations for your expulsion due to misconduct. What's your understanding of this situation?"

**Liam:** "I was shocked when I heard that. I've never been in serious trouble before. I don't know where this is coming from."

**Counselor:** "Have you noticed any changes or had any conflicts recently?"

**Liam:** "Well, there have been tensions with a group of students, but I didn't think it was serious."

**Counselor's Notes:** Liam hints at possible issues with peers. Worth investigating further.

## Interview with Anonymous Student

**Date:** October 1, 2037

**Counselor:** "I understand you wanted to speak confidentially about some concerns."

**Student:** "Yes, I think someone is messing with the records. The students who've been targeted were all involved in bullying others."

**Counselor:** "Can you provide more details?"

**Student:** "I don't want to get in trouble, but they've been making life hard for some of us. Maybe someone decided to take action."

**Counselor's Notes:** This suggests a potential motive related to bullying. Needs discreet investigation.
