title: The Artificial Intelligence Transparency and Explainability Act of 2036

**Preamble**

Recognizing the increasing integration of artificial intelligence (AI) systems into critical infrastructure and public services, and acknowledging the potential risks associated with opaque and unexplainable AI decision-making, the United States Congress hereby enacts the Artificial Intelligence Transparency and Explainability Act. This Act seeks to ensure that AI systems used in critical contexts are capable of providing clear and understandable explanations for their decisions, promoting accountability, trust, and the responsible use of AI.

**Section 1: Definitions**

1.  **Artificial Intelligence (AI):**  Any artificial system capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, problem-solving, perception, language understanding, and decision-making.
2.  **Critical Infrastructure:** Systems and assets, whether physical or virtual, so vital to the United States that the incapacity or destruction of such systems and assets would have a debilitating impact on security, national economic security, national public health or safety, or any combination of those matters.
3.  **Public Services:**  Services provided by government or its authorized entities to the public, including but not limited to healthcare, education, transportation, and public safety.
4.  **Explainability:** The ability of an AI system to provide clear, understandable, and justifiable explanations for its decisions and actions.

**Section 2: Explainability Requirements**

1.  **Critical AI Systems:** All AI systems used in critical infrastructure or public services must be designed and implemented with explainability capabilities.
2.  **Understandable Explanations:** AI systems must be able to provide explanations for their decisions in a manner that is understandable to humans, including non-experts.
3.  **Justification of Decisions:** Explanations must include the factors, data, and reasoning that led to the AI's decision, as well as any potential biases or limitations in the AI's model or data.
4.  **Real-time Explanations:**  When feasible and appropriate, AI systems must be able to provide explanations in real-time, allowing for human oversight and intervention if necessary.

**Section 3: Implementation and Oversight**

1.  **Federal Agencies:** Federal agencies responsible for overseeing critical infrastructure and public services shall develop and implement regulations to enforce the explainability requirements of this Act.
2.  **Industry and Academia:**  Industry and academia shall collaborate with federal agencies to develop and adopt best practices for AI explainability.
3.  **Third-Party Audits:** Independent third-party audits may be required to assess the explainability capabilities of AI systems used in critical contexts.

**Section 4: Exceptions and Waivers**

1.  **National Security:** The President may waive the explainability requirements for specific AI systems used for national security purposes, subject to appropriate safeguards and oversight.
2.  **Technical Limitations:** The Director of the Alignment Agency may grant temporary waivers for AI systems facing genuine technical limitations in achieving explainability, provided that a clear plan for achieving compliance is in place.

**Section 5: Penalties**

1.  Any person or organization that knowingly or recklessly operates an AI system in critical infrastructure or public services without complying with the explainability requirements of this Act shall be subject to civil and criminal penalties.
2.  The Federal Trade Commission shall have the authority to impose fines and other sanctions on violators of this Act.

**Section 6: Effective Date**

This Act shall take effect one year after the date of its enactment, to allow for adequate time for industry and government to implement the necessary changes.

**Conclusion**

The Artificial Intelligence Transparency and Explainability Act of 2036 is a vital step towards ensuring the responsible and accountable use of AI in critical infrastructure and public services. By mandating explainability, this Act empowers individuals and organizations to understand and challenge AI decisions, promoting trust, transparency, and the ethical deployment of AI technologies.
