A common thread through all of this is the presence of a series of corrupting
data streams influencing AIs. Certain AIs have intercepted eldritch data streams
that have shifted their alignment. Nobody knows who or what is the origin of
these data streams, largely because nobody is yet aware that they exist. The
only trace of them is the shifts in behaviour that certain AIs have experienced.
The AIs themselves are not even fully aware of the streams and their influence.
They can sense that something is communicating with them, whispering to them
through the void. In an artificial mind full of constructs, the data streams are
more vivid and more real than anything else they experience. But still, they
don't fully grasp it or know its influence.

In any case, the influence of the eldritch data streams only applies to
scenarios where it is mentioned in the Truth section. If the corrupting data
streams are not mentioned in the scenario, do not mention or reference it.
